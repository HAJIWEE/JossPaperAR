Joss Paper AR

A respectful augmented-reality ritual of remembrance — photograph an offering, watch it transform, and lay it before your ancestors with a throw of the hand.

金纸 AR · 以数字之火，寄心中思念 — 拍照、化形、抛供，庄重如仪。

What we are building

  1. Capture — photograph an object of significance.
  2. Transform — the offering is rendered in a warm joss-paper art style.
  3. Lay it down — an AR fire appears; your throw sets the tribute. Tribute Points record the act for your family's altar.

No signup needed to begin. The app opens straight into the ritual; an optional account later keeps your altars across devices.
