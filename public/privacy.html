1. What we collect

  • Offering photo - your picture to burn. Owner-only.

  • Ancestor names (optional, up to 4) - your tablets only. Never in analytics, crash reports, logs, or ads.
  • Coarse location cell (optional, 4-block area, never precise GPS) - new-ground tribute only, never shown.
  • Account records - tributes, points ledger, streaks, quotas, consent records on your anonymous account.

Never collected: NRIC or ID documents, contact lists, precise GPS trails, payment cards (Google Play Billing handles purchases).
