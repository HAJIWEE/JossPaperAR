## 🎯 Core Concept
An Augmented Reality app that replaces physical joss paper burning with a digital/AR experience, evolving the tradition one step further into abstraction.

### Historical Evolution
1. **Ancient Era**: Emperors and nobility buried valuables with ancestors in tombs
2. **Common Practice**: Poorer civilians burned joss paper as an abstract replacement
3. **Future Vision**: AR/digital abstraction of the ritual (this app)

---

## 🎮 Core Mechanics

### AR Experience Flow
1. **Discovery**: Users travel and discover objects of significance (souvenirs, buildings, places)
2. **Capture**: Snap photos/videos of these items
3. **Cartoonization**: AI transforms objects into abstract/digital versions (KEY TECHNICAL PRIORITY)
4. **Burning Ritual**: AR fire appears on reality, user swipes to throw cartoonized object into the fire
5. **Value Calculation**: Points based on throw accuracy, location, and item frequency

### Key Technical Priorities
- **Cartoonization Style**: Must get the style right - research existing joss paper cartoon styles
- **AR Fire Mechanic**: User-buyable AR object, fixed position on phone (like Pokémon GO's catch mechanic)
- **Throw Accuracy**: 1.2x, 1.5x, 2x multipliers based on throw quality
- **Animation Quality**: Flashier animations for higher value items

---

## 💰 Monetization & Economy

### Freemium Model
- **Points System**: % of value as they burn to honor ancestors
- **Alternative Items**: Users can purchase/exchange standard items (bundles of cash, gold bars) with fixed values when they have nothing to burn
- **AR Fire Collection**: Purely cosmetic - bragging rights, exchangeable designs
- **Monetization Timeline**: 
  - MVP: Free with advertising
  - Medium-term: Advertising revenue
  - Long-term: Cash shop/battle pass system

### Advertising Strategy
- **Family-oriented only**: Travel, shopping apps, government propaganda
- **NO**: Sexual content, gambling content
- **Respectful integration** into the experience

---

## 🏆 Gamification & Progression

### Tribute Points System
- Users know they're progressing when their "Tribute Points" increase on the leaderboard
- More tributes = higher position on leaderboard

### Duolingo-Style Leaderboard
- Show users within similar skill levels to prevent disengagement
- Competition drives engagement without being overwhelming

### Daily Streak System
- Bonus points for longer streaks (similar to other games)
- No forced daily burning to maintain value and reduce server load

### Location-Based Value System
- "Home ground" = where user spends most time burning
- Venturing elsewhere = high value initially, decreases with repeated burns in same vicinity
- Privacy: GPS or signal triangulation, data stored only for ~4 blocks radius

## 👥 Target Audience & Vision

### Primary Users
- **Late 40s-50s age group**
- Families wanting to honor ancestors remotely
- People without access to physical burning locations

### Long-term Vision
- **Family pass-down tradition**: App becomes a legacy that grows over generations
- **Virtual Temple**: Explorable 3D space to decorate like ancestral temple but modern/futuristic (deferred to later versions)
- **Growing over time**: The app and its temples grow as more users join

- ✅ Location-based value system
